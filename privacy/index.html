Privacy

What we collect:
as little as possible.

The short version: this website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and none of our apps require an account.

This website

Pages are served statically. We do not set cookies, do not run a tracking script and do not build a profile of visitors. Server logs kept by the host contain IP addresses and are used only for security and capacity.

Our apps

Games and tools work without an account. Progress and notes are stored on your device. Google Play may collect install and crash data under Google's own policies; the Play listing of each app carries the current data-safety declaration.

Children

None of our apps is directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your requests

To ask what is held about you, or to have it removed, write to mdnexa9@gmail.com. Because we do not run accounts, deleting an app removes its data with it.
