About

A studio that ships,
then keeps quiet.

MD Nexa is an independent software studio. We build puzzle games and small tools, publish them ourselves, and keep the catalogue small enough that every product still gets finished.

What we believe

Software should be understood without a tutorial. If a screen needs an explanation, the screen is wrong. That rule decides what we build and, more often, what we refuse to ship.

Products should work without a connection, because the interesting moment to play is on a train, in a basement, or on a plane. And nothing should require an account before it does its job.

How we work

One product at a time. A game is finished — levels, translations, store page, support — before the next one starts. Updates after launch are corrections and refinements rather than a content treadmill.

Everything is built on Android first because that is where our players are, but the layer under the games is deliberately thin, so a second storefront or a browser build is a wrapper rather than a rewrite.
