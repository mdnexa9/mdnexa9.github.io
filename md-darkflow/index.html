How it plays

Arrows sit on a black board pointing in four directions. Tap one and it slides off if nothing blocks the way; tap the wrong one and you lose a heart. The board is fully visible, so every loss is a mistake you can see coming next time.

How it is built

Levels are authored, not generated, and ordered by the number of moves a clean solve needs. Three hearts per attempt, a hint that refills daily, and no timer anywhere.

What you get

  • Fully visible boards, no guessing
  • Three hearts, no energy timers
  • Hint refills once a day
