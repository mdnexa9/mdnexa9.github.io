MD Work

A day-rate notebook for freelancers. Log the days you worked, pick a currency, and hand a client a clean figure.

Get it on Google PlayAll products
  • Sign-inNot required
  • StorageOn your device

What it does

Set a daily rate and a currency, then mark the days you worked. MD Work keeps the month total, a yearly view and a short summary you can copy into an invoice.

What it does not do

No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your notes stay in the app on your phone, which also means an uninstalled app takes its data with it.

What you get

  • Day and hour rates side by side
  • 28 currencies
  • Nothing leaves your device
