Direction

Android is the start, not the ceiling

The studio is built so the same work can leave Google Play without being rebuilt. These are the three doors we are opening next.

01

Beyond one store

The games are written against a small internal layer, so a second storefront is a new wrapper — not a second codebase.

02

Beyond the phone

Desktop and browser builds are on the table for the puzzle line. The boards already fit any window; the input layer is the work.

03

Beyond one genre

MD Work is the first non-game product. Next are small utilities that share the same rule: one job, done without an account.

The studio

Independent, small, and deliberately slow

MD Nexa is a software studio, not a publisher with a catalogue. The games are free, the tool is paid once, and none of it asks you to create an account before you can use it.

One product at a time

We finish a game before starting the next one. Updates are bugs and refinements, not a content treadmill.

Written by people

Every menu and every store page is written and checked in each of the fourteen languages we ship.

Your data stays yours

No account is needed to play. Nothing is synced to us unless a product says so on its own page.
