Three rules we do not break

They decide what we build and, more often, what we refuse to build.

01

A rule you learn in ten seconds

One screen, one mechanic, no tutorial video. If a level needs explaining, the level is wrong, not the player.

02

Offline before anything else

No spinner between you and the board. Levels work in a plane, a basement or a dead zone.

03

Fourteen languages, by hand

Every menu and store page is written and checked by a person. Machine translation is how a game feels foreign in your own language.

Questions we get

Are the games really free?

Yes. Both are free on Google Play, with no subscription, no trial timer and no account.

Do they work without internet?

Every level works with no connection. Only the optional daily board needs a short connection to load, then it plays offline.

Is there a timer or energy system?

No. You can leave a board half-finished and come back to it later; nothing decays while you are away.

How do I get help?

Write to mdnexa9@gmail.com or use the support page. Replies come from the studio.
