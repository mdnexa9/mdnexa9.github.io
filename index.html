Three rules we do not break

They decide what we build and, more often, what we refuse to build.

01

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no profile, no email wall. You install the app and use it; your records stay in your device storage.

02

Offline by default

A plane, a basement, a dead zone: the app opens and works. Sync is never a precondition for using what you paid nothing for.

03

Translated by people

Fourteen languages, each written and reviewed by hand. Machine-translated menus are how software feels foreign in its own language.

A small studio, on purpose

Questions we get

Are your apps really free?

Yes. Every app and game is free on Google Play. There is no subscription, no trial timer and no account to create.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your device. MD Work stores workdays, earnings and expenses locally; we do not run a server that keeps a copy of your records.

Do they work without internet?

Yes, MD Work and MD Corpses work with no connection at all. In our newer puzzle games only the optional daily challenge needs a short connection.

How do I get help?

Write to mdnexa9@gmail.com or use the support page. Replies come from the studio, usually within two working days.
