MD Bolt Master

Sort every colour onto its own screw until the board is clean. One rule, taught in ten seconds, then a hundred boards that each ask for something slightly different.

How it plays

Bolts arrive stacked in colours. You move a nut onto the screw of the same colour; a screw holds only its own. Levels grow by adding colours, tighter spacing and a few parts that need to be freed before others can move.

How it is built

The board is generated from a solved state, so a level can never be impossible. Stars are earned per solve and spent on hints; the daily board is the same for every player and resets at local midnight.

What you get

  • A daily board shared by every player
  • Stars for hints, earned by solving, never sold
  • Written by hand in fourteen languages
