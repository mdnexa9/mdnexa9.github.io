MD Corpses

A climbing game played with one finger: reach a little further each run and learn the rhythm of the mountain.

How it plays

Hold to grip, release to drop, and time the grab as the surface moves. Runs are short on purpose: you either reach the next ledge or you learn why you missed it.

How it is built

Hand-tuned levels rather than an endless generator, so difficulty is deliberate. Nothing is locked behind an advert or a waiting timer.

What you get

  • One-finger control
  • Short, repeatable runs
  • No ad gates
