How we build

Three decisions repeated in every product.

We do not start from a feature list. We start from one question: what is the smallest thing that makes this product genuinely useful? We build that, use it, then add one step at a time.

The result is apps that open fast, read clearly on the first screen, and work without an internet connection or an account. When something breaks, we fix the cause instead of covering it with an interface.

Read our articles Privacy policy

Principles

Simplicity before features
We delete a step before we add a button. If a screen needs a long explanation, the design is not finished.
Privacy by design
No accounts and no subscriptions for core use. Your records stay on your device, and we collect as little as possible.
Arabic first, not translated
We write Arabic as it is spoken, then add English. Same logic in every language, never a word-for-word copy.
Honest early releases
We ship when the product works, and we describe exactly what it does — no promises about features that do not exist yet.
