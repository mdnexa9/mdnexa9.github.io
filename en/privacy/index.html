Privacy Policy Your records stay yours

This policy covers mdnexa.online and the MD Work Android app. It describes what actually happens — which data is stored, where, and who can see it. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

1. Scope

This policy applies to this website (mdnexa.online) and to the MD Work Android app, published on Google Play as app.mdwork.notebook. The separate game MD Corpses has its own policy, published on the MD Corpses privacy policy page.

The data controller is the MD Nexa studio, reachable at mdnexa9@gmail.com.

2. No account, and your work records are not uploaded

MD Work has no sign-in and no account, and it does not ask for your name, email or phone number to work. The days you record, half days, pay, expenses, notes, reminders, the timer and the checklist are stored inside the app's storage on your device.

Those records are never sent to our servers. We hold no copy, cannot read them, and cannot recover them for you. Deleting a record in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may copy app data into your own Google account. That copy is governed by your Google account and settings, not by us, and it may remain after you uninstall the app until you delete it from your backups.

Reports you export (a CSV file or an image) are created on your device and never pass through us. If you choose to share one with another app, that app receives it under its own policy.

3. Ads in MD Work

MD Work is free and supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob. To serve and measure an ad and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate city or region is derived), device type, OS version and language, and your interaction with the ad.

Your work records are not sent to Google for advertising, and we do not sell your data. Ad data is processed under Google's policies: how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Depending on your country, a consent screen may appear before ads; your choice there determines whether data is used for personalisation. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads under Settings → Google → Ads. Turning personalisation off does not stop ads from appearing; it makes them less tied to your interests.

4. The website: analytics and cookies

We use Google Tag Manager (GTM-T6RJDZLR) to load Google Analytics on the site's pages. It records information such as the page you visited, the page that referred you, browser, device and language, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and clicks on Google Play links. The purpose is to see which pages are read and which are not — not to identify you.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) and browser storage entries to distinguish sessions. We do not link this data to a name or email, and we do not display ads on the site.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the hosting provider may keep technical request logs (including IP addresses) for security and operations.

You can stop or reduce this measurement in your browser: block or delete cookies, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site keeps working in full.

5. When you contact us

Contact happens by email or WhatsApp. We receive what you send: your email address or phone number and your message. We use it only to answer and follow up, keep it while the conversation is open, and delete it if you ask from the same address. WhatsApp is a Meta service, and Meta's policy applies to messages sent through it.

6. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it for marketing, or collect your contacts, photos, files or precise location. The only sharing happens when you choose to share a report, or when your browser requests an external service itself (Google, GitHub, WhatsApp).

7. Security

Your records live on your device, and their protection depends on your screen lock, device encryption and Android. There is no way for us to restore a record lost with the device or deleted from the app, because we hold no copy. No storage or transmission method is one hundred percent secure, so keep a screen lock and export your monthly report when the number matters.

8. Retention and deletion

In the app: records remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. A copy may remain in device backups until you delete it from your account.

On the site: analytics data is processed inside Google according to the retention settings of our analytics account. Messages you send us remain until you ask us to delete them.

To delete: remove each record in the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → MD Work → Storage → Clear data. To delete your messages, write to us from the same address with the word "delete".

9. Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, or object to its processing. In practice we hold only your messages; work records are on your device and under your control. Write to mdnexa9@gmail.com and we will reply within a reasonable time.

You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

10. Children

The site and MD Work are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we make a material change to how we handle data, we update this page and the date at the top, and publish the change here before it takes effect. Continuing to use the site or the app after an update means you have seen the published version.
