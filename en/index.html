The studio

About MD Nexa

MD Nexa (also written “مك نكسا”, pronounced em-dee NEK-sa) is an independent studio building small, fast Android apps and games. We start from a real problem in the workday, or from a game idea you can play with one finger, then polish it and publish it on Google Play in Arabic and English.

No accounts and no subscriptions in your way: MD Work keeps workdays, earnings and expenses on your device, and MD Corpses is a climb out of a well built from your own failures. MD Bolt Master and MD Darkflow are in development.

Frequently asked

What is MD Nexa?
An independent Android studio that builds apps and games and publishes them on Google Play. Its released products are MD Work and MD Corpses.
Is MD Work free?
Yes. MD Work is free on Google Play, needs no account, and keeps your work, earnings and expense records on your own device.
Do the apps support Arabic?
Yes. MD Work and MD Corpses both work in Arabic and English, and MD Corpses ships 21 languages.
