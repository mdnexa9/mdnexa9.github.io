The studio

About MD Nexa

MD Nexa (also written “مك نكسا”, pronounced em-dee NEK-sa) is an independent software company building original games, digital platforms and services, and new projects. We start from a real problem in the workday or a game idea you can play with one finger, then polish it and publish it on Google Play and the web in Arabic and English.

No accounts and no subscriptions in your way: MD Work keeps workdays, earnings and expenses on your device, and MD Corpses is a climb out of a well built from your own failures. MD Bolt Master and MD Darkflow are in development.
