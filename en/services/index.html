How we work

Four steps, and you always know where you are.

We do not start with code. We start with a short call that produces a written scope: what is built, what is not, and how success is measured. That scope is the agreement, and anything added later is quoted separately and clearly.

Then a prototype you can click and try, weekly stages that are delivered and reviewed, and finally launch and full handover. Every week you know what shipped and what remains.

How we build Free guides

The steps

1. Written scope
One call produces a document: the goal, the core screens, what is out of scope, and the acceptance test.
2. Prototype
A clickable, testable version before the full build, so wrong assumptions surface before launch.
3. Weekly build
A working version every week with written notes, documented decisions, and room for change inside the scope.
4. Launch & handover
Store or hosting release, plus source code, documentation and publishing accounts in your name.

Direct answers

Who owns the code after delivery?
You do: full source with build documentation, and no technical lock or dependency on us.
How long does it take?
A prototype in two to four weeks; the full release in stages that each ship on their own, with the timeline written into the scope.
How is the price set?
A fixed price per stage after a written scope. Additions are quoted separately before they are built.
Can you work with our team?
Yes, on a defined part of an existing product, with weekly delivery and shared code review.
What comes after launch?
A fix window, then optional monthly support. You can continue with any other team because everything is documented.
