What MD Work does

MD Work is a simple replacement for spreadsheets and paper notes for people paid by the day: workshops, building sites, and any job tracked in days or hours. Open it, log the day, close it.

Every record stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no internet needed — and you can review the whole month before the work period ends.

Features

Workday tracking

Record a full day, a half day or a day off, with a note for each.

Earnings and expenses

Follow what you earned and what you spent, and see what is left.

Daily and monthly reports

Read your records by day or by month.

Quick tools

Quick note, timer and checklist in the same app.

Private by design

No account, main records on your device, Arabic and English interface.

Is MD Work free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, needs no account, and keeps your records on your own device.

Does it need internet?

No. You can log days, earnings and expenses offline.

Can it track half days?

Yes — full day, half day or day off, with totals by day or month.
