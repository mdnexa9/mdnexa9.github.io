Quick answers

How do I contact support?
WhatsApp on +39 376 144 3811 or email mdnexa9@gmail.com. We reply within one working day, Monday to Saturday.
Is MD Work free and account-free?
Yes, free on Google Play and no account needed. Your records stay on your device and you can export them.
Where is my MD Work data stored?
On your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the records, so export a backup first.
How do I move data to a new phone?
Export the file from MD Work on the old phone, then import it on the new one after installing.
What do you need for an MD Corpses bug report?
Phone model, Android version, game version, a short description, and a screenshot if possible.
Do the apps support Arabic?
Yes, MD Work and MD Corpses work in Arabic and English, and MD Corpses ships 21 languages.
