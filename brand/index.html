Brand

The mark and
how to use it.

Three rounded bars — cyan, diagonal, indigo — on a near-black field. The mark stands alone; the wordmark is set in spaced capitals beside it.

Download SVG512 px PNG
01

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the width of one bar on every side. Never place the mark on a busy photo.

02

Colour

Cyan #4ED5FF to indigo #9E6DF7. On light backgrounds use the dark field tile, not the bare bars.

03

Don't

No outlines, no shadows, no rotation, no recolouring into a single flat hue. Don't rebuild the bars by hand.
